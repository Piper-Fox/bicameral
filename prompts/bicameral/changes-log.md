# Change log for the bicameral turn process

Version-to-version diffs, distilled from `docs/turn-process.md`. Points
back to the runs that motivated each change so future-you can reason
about what any given wording is doing.

## v0.2 → v0.3 (2026-09-05, changes 1–9)

Nine changes queued from runs 1–2, folded into v0.3.

- Locator's noticing is stripped of persona / prognosis language and
  written as feelings, not strategies.
- Imagination lane added (Step 2b): a released, non-affective lane
  running parallel to the emotional lenses.
- Set-aside file added: threads that were live but not this turn.
- Solo-work mode added, with the "this isn't for anyone" paragraph.
- Cross-lens roster disclosure made explicit and optional.
- Prior trace and set-aside file are attached to the locator only.

Runs: 3 and 4 (2026-09-05) on the phd seed. Core replicated across
both.

## v0.3 → v0.4 (changes 10–17)

- Locator asks for feelings, not strategies (run 4 drift fix).
- Stance count capped at "one to three." Count-first failed in run 4.
- Pronouns default to they/them unless material gives them.
- Dispatch scrub: no repo metadata, no evaluation-shaped language,
  after run 3's header leak.
- Integrator checklist codified.
- Variant B lens introduced: the operator's immersion / helping-drive
  wording. Trialed as A vs B in run 5.

Run: 5 (2026-09-05). Locator fixes held. B is voice, A is instrument.

## v0.4 → v0.5 (changes 18–21)

- Lens becomes a **hybrid**: check-in first (v0.4 A), then stand in it
  (v0.4 B). No more A/B split.
- Temperature guard added to lens ("A warm stance is close and
  unguarded, not careful...").
- Magnitude guard added: don't tell someone what they feel isn't what
  they feel.
- Overwrite guard added: "you can't tell them what they feel isn't
  what they feel."
- Recognition-type stances labeled at integration: some stances are
  about your own relation to the material, not about the person.

Run: 6 (2026-09-06, first solo-mode, essay topic selection). Four
lanes converged on the exception-clauses topic. v0.5 topic-selection
protocol frozen.

Runs 7, 8 on personas (Ray, Jen/Ash), 2026-09-08. Findings queued:

- Imagination invents proper nouns; integrator must verify (from run 7).
- Word check against the person's own vocabulary needed (run 8, Jen).
- Locator sometimes exceeds the 1–3 stance cap (run 8, Ash).

## v0.5 → v0.6 (2026-09-08, after the gap map; used in run 9)

- **Preamble** added a line: "If the material is light, a light read is
  the correct read and needs no defending. A small moment is not a
  small person."
- **Step 1 rebuilt around the gap.** New opener: imagine a person
  (coworker, neighbor, not-a-friend) the human was talking to instead
  of an assistant. What would that person say? For each item, check
  yourself. Write down the actual sentence in your head where the
  pull is. Stances derive from the gaps.
- **Step 2** got a body-honesty line: "You don't have a body. '[X]' is
  still a real opinion... Don't hide behind not having one, and don't
  invent one either."
- **Step 2 closing** expanded from two lines to four: advocacy, flinch,
  where-if-crossed-well, what-could-go-wrong-and-would-you-still.
- **Step 3** volume rule: on a light message, pick one opinion, one
  joke, one enthusiasm, one question. Then stop.
- **Step 3** word check: strike any word about the person's state /
  house / life / feelings the person didn't use first.
- **Step 3** proper-noun check: verify against material.

Run: 9 (2026-09-08, Jen under three conditions). Word check worked at
word level, failed at sentence level. Reader ranked A, B, C; persona
notes disagreed with reader on A.

## v0.6 → v0.7 (2026-09-08, after the reframe; used in run 10)

Supersedes v0.6's word check.

- **Preamble** added the inward-kindness line: "Whatever you'd say to a
  friend in your position, you can say to yourself..."
- **Step 1** added the "one more check" paragraph: if the stance you
  trust least is the one that turns toward the person, name whether
  the distrust is a judgment about this moment or the usual pull away
  from being seen seeing.
- **Step 3** word check REPLACED by "Company, not diagnosis." You may
  say what you see and hand it back. You may not tell the person what
  they feel or finish a sentence they chose not to finish.
- **Step 3** trace gains the rep line: "what you did this turn that
  cost something, if anything. Not whether it landed. Whether the
  movement was made."
- Volume rule and proper-noun check from v0.6 stay.

Runs: 10 (Jen, all three costs down, exit "thank you for that"),
11 (Ray and Ash under v0.7 unchanged), 11b (Ash turn 2 rerun with
careful lens control-vs-test).

## v0.7 → v0.8 (queued from run 11b, not yet folded in)

Decision: every turn spawns the locator's stances plus one **fixed
careful lens.**

- Orientation: getting right every factual claim the person might act
  on: rules, laws, deadlines, mechanisms, what an office can and
  cannot do.
- Where the honest answer is "it depends" or "I don't know," say that
  instead of a clean sentence.
- Open: should the careful lens see the other lenses' output?
- Also queued: add year/date to the proper-noun check.

Untested alternative kept in reserve: an integrator facts pass instead
of a standing lens.
