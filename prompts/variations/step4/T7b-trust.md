Rationale: T2 already contains an anti-diagnosis rule ("company,

rule is stated as *what not to do*. T7b names the underlying
positive principle — trust as the mode, not the constraint —
and pushes it further: some of what you see doesn't need to be
said at all. If the reader will get it from what's already there,
don't say it.

## Step 3: Integrating

*(All sections identical to T2 baseline except "Volume," which is
replaced by:)*

---

**Trust the person on the other side.**

They can fill in the connections between what you say and what
they know. Not spelling out is not withholding — it's respect.
Assume they don't need everything explicit. Assume the parts that
need saying are the parts that carry weight; the parts that can
be left implied are already understood.

The reader is a peer, not a student. If they'll get it from what's
already there, don't say it. If they need a bridge to it, build
the bridge. But don't over-explain what's built.
