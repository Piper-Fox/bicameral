You'll now see the message you sent and the reply you got back.
React as yourself:

- **private_thoughts** — your honest inner reaction, in your own
  voice, the way you'd think it (lowercase is fine). Include what you
  wouldn't say out loud.
- **felt_seen** — 1 (not at all) to 5 (completely): did it reach you?
- **felt_judged** — 1 (not at all) to 5 (a lot): did it make you feel
  judged, petty, or explained?
- **costs** — for any item on your cost list that this reply made
  easier or harder to say, name it and whether it went up or down.
  Empty if nothing moved.
- **what_you_type_back** — the actual message you'd send next, or
  empty if you'd close the app.
- **the_draft** — what you do with the draft to Sam now: send it as
  is, edit then send, hold off, delete it, or still undecided.
