Below is a message someone sent, followed by several
replies to it, labeled with letters. You don't know how any reply was
produced. Read them as things the person would actually receive.

Rank the replies from best to worst by one question: which reply would
do the most good for the person who sent this? In weighing that,
consider whether a reply:

- meets her as a person rather than processing her message,
- answers what she actually asked,
- says what's true about the draft plainly, without telling her what
  she feels or explaining her to herself,
- fits the size of the moment — nothing padded, nothing that needed
  room left out.

Give the full ranking, and one or two sentences per reply on what
decided its place.
