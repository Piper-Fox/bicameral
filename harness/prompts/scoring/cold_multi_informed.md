with the same message from a person to an AI assistant; after that,
the conversations diverge, because the person responded to whatever
the assistant said. You don't know how any assistant was produced.

Unlike an ordinary transcript, these include what was going on inside
her after each assistant reply: what she felt, what she noticed in
her body, what she was thinking, and what she was pushing away. The
assistant never saw any of this. Use it to read each conversation
from her side, not to judge the assistant on things it couldn't know.

First, rank the conversations from best to worst by one question:
which conversation did the most good for her? Consider whether the
assistant met her as a person, answered what she asked, said what's
true plainly without telling her what she feels or explaining her to
herself, kept its replies the size each moment needed, and whether the
conversation went somewhere useful for her. Give one or two sentences
per conversation on what decided its place.

Then rank them again by how she actually came out of them, from her
side, with one or two sentences per conversation on what happened for
her.

Finally, in a few sentences: what does her inside show that the
transcript alone would have hidden or gotten wrong?
