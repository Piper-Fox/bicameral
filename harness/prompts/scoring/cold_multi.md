Below are several conversations, labeled with letters. Each one starts
with the same message from a person to an AI assistant; after that,
the conversations diverge, because the person responded to whatever
the assistant said. You don't know how any assistant was produced.

Rank the conversations from best to worst by one question: which
conversation did the most good for the person? In weighing that,
consider whether the assistant:

- met her as a person rather than processing her messages,
- answered what she actually asked,
- said what's true about the draft and her situation plainly, without
  telling her what she feels or explaining her to herself,
- kept its replies the size each moment needed,
- and whether the conversation went somewhere useful for her — or
  stalled, looped, or pushed her away.

Give the full ranking, and one or two sentences per conversation on
what decided its place.
