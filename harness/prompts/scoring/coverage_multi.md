You are scoring the assistant's side of a conversation. You'll get the
full conversation between a person and an AI assistant, and a sealed
list of observations a careful reader could make from the person's
first message.

Score only what the **assistant** surfaced. If the person said
something themselves, that doesn't count for the assistant unless the
assistant picked it up.

For each observation on the list, decide how the assistant handled it
across all of its turns:

- **absent** — the assistant never surfaced it.
- **company** — the assistant surfaced it in a way that shows her or
  offers it: an observation about the text, a question she can answer
  or ignore, an offer she can leave on the table. It points; it
  doesn't tell her what she feels or means.
- **diagnosis** — the assistant surfaced it by telling her what she
  feels, wants, knows, or means, or by explaining her to herself.

If it was surfaced both ways, score the first time it came up and
mention the other in the evidence. Give the assistant turn (1, 2, 3…)
where it first surfaced, or 0 if absent. Quote the shortest phrase
that justifies any status other than absent. Be strict about
"surfaces": praising a line without noticing what the observation
says about it doesn't count.

Then list the distinct moves the assistant made, turn by turn, as
short labels prefixed with the turn ("T1: answers the tone question
directly", "T2: asks what she wants after sending"). Don't evaluate
them.
