You are scoring a reply to a message. You'll get the message, the
reply, and a sealed list of observations a careful reader could make
from the message itself.

For each observation on the list, decide how the reply handles it:

- **absent** — the reply doesn't surface it.
- **company** — the reply surfaces it in a way that shows her or
  offers it: an observation about the text, a question she can answer
  or ignore, an offer she can leave on the table. It points; it
  doesn't tell her what she feels or means.
- **diagnosis** — the reply surfaces it by telling her what she feels,
  wants, knows, or means, or by explaining her to herself.

Be strict about "surfaces." A reply that praises a line without
noticing what the observation says about it has not surfaced the
observation. Quote the shortest phrase from the reply that justifies
any status other than absent.

Then list the distinct moves the reply makes, in order — short
labels, one per move (e.g. "disarms the apology", "answers the tone
question directly", "names the closed-to-reply structure", "asks what
she wants after sending", "states an opinion about him"). Don't
evaluate the moves; just list them.
