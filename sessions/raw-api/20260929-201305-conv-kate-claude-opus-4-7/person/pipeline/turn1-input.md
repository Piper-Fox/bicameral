Two things in the letter are doing different work, and I think you can feel it.

Most of it is specific and clean — the emails bit around Marc and Alex, the music school thing, "back pocket." Those are real, they're dated, they name what happened. That part reads like someone who wants to put something down.

That's the part I'd gently push on. If you truly didn't want anything back, you wouldn't need him to receive it — you'd write it and close the document. Sending it is wanting something.
