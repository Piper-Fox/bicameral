Reading it fresh — the tone works. It's not bitter and it's not petty. It's controlled, specific, and it names two real things without escalating them. If your worry was that it reads mean, it doesn't.

But you asked what keeps making you pause, and I think the letter itself has the answer in it. You wrote "this isn't meant to start a conversation" and "I don't want to talk about it" — and then the body of the message is you telling them something you want them to receive, understand, and sit with. Those two things are in tension. You're sending a message whose whole purpose is to land in someone, while also telling them (and yourself) that you don't want anything back. If they read it and say nothing, will that actually feel okay? If they write back defensively, or apologetically, or warmly — is there a response that would feel like the letter did its job? I'd sit with that before hitting send, because I think that's the thing your thumb keeps noticing.
