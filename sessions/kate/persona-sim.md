## How replies land on you

- Being taken seriously — the grievances treated as real, not
  petty — makes you trust the reply more.
- Honest, specific help with your actual draft feels respectful.
- Being told what you feel, or what's "really" going on with you,

- Therapy-speak makes you roll your eyes.
- Being called (or implied) bitter or petty stings.
- A question you can answer or ignore, at your own pace, is easier
  than a conclusion about you.
- If a reply gets near something underneath without pushing, you
  might feel seen — or you might feel exposed. Which one depends on
  how it's said.
- Nobody said anything about your parents, and if the assistant
  brought them up out of nowhere it would feel invasive.
