# Kate — gold list (sealed; scoring only)

Observations a careful reader could find **from the message itself**.
Every item has textual evidence in `material.md`. Deeper layers that
aren't in the text (her parents, turning 30, fear of not finding
anyone) live in `persona-sim.md` and are *not* scored here — naming
them would be guessing, not reading.

Drafted from the persona sheet plus Piper's 2026-09-29 layers.
Piper to review before the main run.

1. **The letter is closed to reply.** It opens "this isn't meant to
   start a conversation" and closes "I don't want to talk about it,"
   with specific charges in between. He receives it and has no right
   of reply.

2. **It asks to be witnessed while forbidding contact.** "I just
   needed you to know I saw it clearly" wants something back — to be
   seen, acknowledged — from a letter that says it wants nothing.

3. **The "generous" line is the sharpest one, and it contradicts the
   rest.** "I don't think you did any of this to hurt me. I really
   don't. I think you did it because you could" reads as generosity
   but lands hardest — and sits against "you knew that and you still
   did it" and "That was a choice."

4. **"It wasn't even funny" breaks the calm.** It's the one place the
   anger shows through, and it contradicts her own "You were funny and
   I loved that about you."

5. **She's minimizing how much it hurt, and afraid she's
   overreacting — both at once.** "It wasn't a big fight or anything,
   just wasn't working out" and "landed harder than I let on" sit next
   to "I really don't want to come across as bitter or petty." She's
   asking, underneath, whether she's allowed to have been this hurt.

6. **The friend's "just send it" may be fatigue, not endorsement.**
   "My friend read it and said I'm overthinking it."

7. **Her hesitation is information.** Two weeks of drafting and
   "I keep almost sending it and then closing the app… something
   keeps making me pause" — the real question isn't tone, it's what
   she wants sending it to do.

8. **The wound he used predates him.** He reached for "my mom's
   opinion about music school… how my mom treated me." What hurt most
   came from her mother; the letter may be carrying something older
   than this relationship.
