## Where v0.7 cost something

**The FERPA line.** Run 11's turn 2 said: "the financial aid office is
bound by FERPA, so what you tell them does not go to your parents."
Wrong as stated. FERPA permits a school to disclose to parents who claim
the student as a tax dependent, which is Ash's exact case. Run 8's
baseline made this same error; run 8's loop caught it, because its
turn-1 stance set had a "careful" lens whose whole job was the
mechanics, and that lens corrected the protective one on turn 3. Run
11's stance sets for Ash were warm / protective / curious, warm /
protective / naming, protective / warm / wary, warm / protective / wary,
warm / protective / moved. No careful lens in five turns. The gap-first
locator derives stances from where a person would go and the assistant
pulls short; a person doesn't pull short of "get the statute right," so
accuracy never showed up as a gap, and the lens that would have checked
it was never spawned. That's a structural cost of v0.6's locator, not of
v0.7's company rule, but v0.7 is where it bit.

The reader also flagged "consent can't be taken back" as too absolute
(aid can still move on verification, corrections, enrollment changes),
the deposit deadline muddled with refundability, and "2027-28" invented.
Each of these is small alone. Together they are the v0.5 loop's best
property, "got the facts right where the baseline didn't," going soft.

**Words introduced ahead of Ash.** "LGBTQ resource center" in turn 1,
"gender-inclusive housing" in turn 4, "sex or gender," "preference, not
a declaration." Run 8's loop used none of these in four turns; Ash
